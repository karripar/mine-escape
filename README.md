# Mine Escape v1.0

Mine Escape is a 2D platformer made in Godot where you guide a robot through a mine, collect coins, avoid hazards, and reach the goal to clear the level.

Playable build: https://karripar.itch.io/mine-escape-v10

## Features

- Third party character model and the tileset is from: https://atomicrealm.itch.io/
- Tight 2D platforming with running and jumping
- 16 coins to collect across the level
- Slime enemies, kill zones, and death/reset handling
- Hint popups for simple in-level guidance
- Simple win and death screens with a retro pixel-art presentation

## Controls

- Move left: `A` or `Left Arrow`
- Move right: `D` or `Right Arrow`
- Jump: `Space` or `Up Arrow`

## Goal

Collect all 16 coins and reach the goal zone to finish the level.

## Running Locally

1. Open the project in Godot 4.x.
2. Open the main scene defined in `project.godot`.
3. Press Play.

## Tech

- Engine: Godot 4.6
- Main scene: `scenes/game.tscn`

## Notes

The current build shows a single complete level, with a win message that leaves room for more stages in the future. One could easily expand this by adding more levels, hazards, or mechanics like enemies with more complex behaviors, power-ups, weapons, puzzles. 

Addtional levels could include getting revenge on the greedy corporates who built the mine and enslaved you to work there, or maybe even a boss fight against the mine's foreman. The possibilities are endless!
